## Free Group Teaser

I am building a caption system because I am tired of pretending auto captions are good enough.

The problem is not generating captions.

The problem is trusting them after the model gets a name wrong, misses an acronym, mangles a sentence, or highlights a word late enough to feel cheap.

So the new system is being built around transcript correction, forced alignment, active-word captions, proof-frame QA, and a memory loop that learns from real fixes.

This is the kind of overbuilt workflow that makes sense once you have posted enough videos with captions that were almost right.
