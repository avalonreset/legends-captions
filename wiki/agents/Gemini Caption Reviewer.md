# Gemini Caption Reviewer

Gemini is useful as a second-pass reviewer for visual proof frames and ambiguity checks.

Gemini should focus on:

- text visible in screenshots;
- whether highlighted words match likely speech;
- whether model names and acronyms look right;
- whether a moment should be escalated to human review.
