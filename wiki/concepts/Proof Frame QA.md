# Proof Frame QA

Proof-frame QA catches mistakes that command output cannot see.

For every final captioned render, inspect frames from the actual video file. A screenshot from the ASS preview is not enough. The frame should confirm text content, line breaks, bubble placement, active color, and whether the caption belongs to the spoken moment.
